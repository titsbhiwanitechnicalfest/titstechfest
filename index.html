About The Fest

A Nexus of Science, Technology & Culture

Rooted in a historic legacy of excellence, TIT Techfest 2026 brings together dynamic minds from schools and colleges to ideate, build, and celebrate.

The Technological Institute of Textile & Sciences

Established in 1943 under the visionary guidance of the Birla Education Trust, TIT&S Bhiwani has been an illustrious pioneer in technical education, engineering innovation, and research.

TIT Techfest 2026 embodies this eight-decade heritage by offering dual competition streams: fostering scientific inquiry among school students and challenging college technocrats with industry-grade prototypes, startup visions, and street drama.

TIT Techfest features dual dedicated categories:
• School Section: Open to students of Classes 11th&12th for both technical (Science Model, Quiz, Poster, Debate, Essay) and cultural events (Singing & Dance, Hindi & English Poetry, Mono Acting, Solo Folk Dance).
• College Section: Open to all undergraduate/postgraduate engineering, diploma, polytechnic, science, and arts students (B.Tech, M.Tech, BCA, MCA, B.Sc, etc.).
